## Overview

A Christmas holiday trip to Hawaii. We spent 4 days at Maui and 4 days at Big Island.

I spent time searching for the Hawaii endemic birds, and saw 6 of them:

- Hawaiian Goose
- Hawaiian Coot
- Maui Alauahio
- Apapane
- Iiwi
- Hawaii Amakihi

[eBird trip report](https://ebird.org/tripreport/155019).
