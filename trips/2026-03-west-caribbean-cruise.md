## Overview

A West Caribbean cruise trip on Star Princess. The cruise visited Honduras (Roatán), Belize and Mexico (Cozumel). It has been 10 years since my last cruise trip and is the first cruise trip of my kids.

### Tue, March 24 — Roatán, Honduras

- Morning birding at the cruise terminal
- **Tutu & Logan:** Dolphin tour at Anthony’s Key, 10:00am, 45 minutes
- Daniel Johnson's Monkey and Sloth Hangout
    - Saw many endemic De Queiroz's Spiny-tailed Iguanas there
- Notes
    - Port hours: 7:00am–4:30pm
    - We took a taxi at the port. It cost $130 for four people for a whole day.

### Wed, March 25 — Belize City, Belize

- **Laogong:** Lamanai Mayan Ruins Tour
    - Booked from the cruise
    - Birding was my main objective
- **Tutu, Logan & Luca:** Belizean Bits & Bites Food Tastings and City Tour in Belize, 10:00am–12:00pm
- Notes
    - Port hours: 7:00am–5:00pm
    - A 30-min water shuttle ride is required between the cruise and the port, and the last water shuttle to the cruise departs at 4:00pm.

### Thu, March 26 — Cozumel, Mexico

- **Laogong & Luca:**: Birding at Planetario Cha'an Ka'an de Cozumel
    - The parking lot is an excellent place for the Cozumel endemics.
    - Found Black Catbird and Cozumel Emerald there
- **Tutu & Logan:** 9:15am, Stingray tour
- Punta Sur Eco Beach Park
    - For the endemic Pygmy Raccoon
- Notes
    - Port hours: 8:00am–6:00pm
    - We rented a car at Fiesta Car Rental Puerta Maya.
