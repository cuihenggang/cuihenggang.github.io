## Overview

A Christmas holiday trip to the Bahamas. We had a tour on the Exuma island and then spent time on the New Providence island. After the Bahamas trip, we spent another week in Miami, which is not on this trip report.

[eBird trip report](https://ebird.org/tripreport/155007).

## Daily Itinerary

### Mon, December 20 — Arrival in Exuma

- Fly to George Town, Exuma (GGT)
    - Flight: AA 3884, 3:47pm–4:59pm

Stay: Hideaways Exuma

### Tue, December 21 — Aquaquest Escapes Tour

- Aquaquest Escapes Tour
    - Snorkeling at plane wreck
    - Snorkeling at Thunderball Grotto
    - Swimming with nurse sharks
    - Swimming with pigs

Stay: Hideaways Exuma

### Wed, December 22 — The Retreat National Park

- Fly from George Town (GGT) to Nassau (NAS)
    - Flight: UP 371, 8:15am–9:00am
- Birding at The Retreat National Park
    - We took a taxi from the hotel to get there, but it was not easy to get a taxi back. The driver charged $40 for a 15-min ride.

Stay: Comfort Suites Paradise Island

### Thu, December 23 — Relax on the Paradise Island

- Atlantis Bahamas

Stay: Comfort Suites Paradise Island

### Fri, December 24 — Flight to Miami

- Fly from Nassau (NAS) to Miami (MIA)
    - Flight: AA 4022, 4:34pm–5:46pm

Stay: Best Western Plus Miami Airport North Hotel & Suites
