## Overview

A Christmas holiday trip to Egypt. We booked the tour with 埃及豆豆, and the tour guide, Mimo, can speak very fluent Chinese. We visited the Pyramids of Giza in Cairo, took an overnight train to Aswan, cruised along the Nile to Luxor, and ended our trip in Hurghada. I did some birding on the Nile River cruise.

[eBird trip report](https://ebird.org/tripreport/155001).
