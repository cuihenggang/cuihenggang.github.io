## Overview

A Christmas holiday trip to Aruba and Colombia. In Colombia, we visited Medellín, Río Claro and Bogotá.

- [eBird trip report for Aruba](https://ebird.org/tripreport/185970)
- [eBird trip report for Colombia](https://ebird.org/tripreport/185971)


## Daily Itinerary

### Mon, December 18 — Overnight in Atlanta

- Fly to Atlanta, 16:52–18:45
- Take the ATL SkyTrain to the hotel

Stay: SpringHill Suites by Marriott Atlanta Airport Gateway, Atlanta

### Tue, December 19 — Arrival in Aruba

- Fly to Aruba, 07:55–12:55
- Alamo car rental
- Pick up Tutu's birthday cake at Bright Bakery

Stay: Renaissance Wind Creek Aruba Resort, Aruba

### Wed, December 20 — Renaissance Island & Aruba wildlife

- Donkey Sanctuary Aruba
- Aruba Aloe Factory
- Bubali Bird Sanctuary
- Malmok Beach
- Zeerover Restaurant

Stay: Renaissance Wind Creek Aruba Resort, Aruba

### Thu, December 21 — Exploring Aruba

- Morning birding at Spaans Lagoon
- Ayo Rock Formations
- Aruba Ostrich Farm
- Natural Bridge
- California Lighthouse

Stay: Renaissance Wind Creek Aruba Resort, Aruba

### Fri, December 22 — Aruba to Medellín

- Renaissance Island
- Fly to Medellín, 17:40–21:44

Stay: Movich Hotel Las Lomas, Medellín

### Sat, December 23 — Medellín

- 09:00: Localiza car rental
- 13:00–15:00: Meet Roberto Escobar Tour
- Pueblito Paisa

Stay: GHL Hotel Portón Medellín, Medellín

### Sun, December 24 — Chocolate, birding & Comuna 13

- **Tutu & Logan:** Chocolate tour with Medellin Day Trips, 07:00–13:00
- **Laogong:** Native Birding Colombia tour, 05:40–noon
    - Highlights include Andean Motmot, Southern Emerald-Toucanet, etc.
- 14:00–17:00: Graffiti tour in Comuna 13, Medellín

Stay: GHL Hotel Portón Medellín, Medellín

### Mon, December 25 — Guatapé

- Guatapé

Stay: GHL Hotel Portón Medellín, Medellín

### Tue, December 26 — Río Claro

- Drive to Río Claro
- Restaurante El Palacio De Los Frijoles
    - Good birding place on the way
    - We also saw Silver-brown Tamarin here
- Río Claro Reserve
    - The Oilbird Cave (5.886938, -74.85272) is about 20 minutes walking from the lodge
    - Oilbirds fly out from the cave every evening at about 18:15

Stay: Los Colores Ecoparque, Río Claro

### Wed, December 27 — Río Claro to Bogotá

- Birding at Los Colores Ecoparque
- Drive to Bogotá
- Mount Monserrate

Stay: Hyatt Place Bogota/Convention Center, Bogotá

### Thu, December 28 — Birding & Bogotá city tour

- **Laogong:** Birdwatching day trip at Chicaque Natural Park, 07:00–15:00
- **Tutu & Logan:** Bogotá city tour, 08:00–15:00, including La Candelaria, with optional Monserrate and Gold Museum visits
- 18:00: Dinner at Humo Negro Restaurant

Stay: Hyatt Place Bogota/Convention Center, Bogotá

### Fri, December 29 — La Florida Park & Salt Cathedral

- La Florida Park
    - A good place to find the endemic Bogotá Rail
    - I heard it but unfortunately didn't see it
- Salt Cathedral
- Return the car before 19:00

Stay: Hyatt Place Bogota/Convention Center, Bogotá

### Sat, December 30 — Departure

- Fly back, 09:00–18:46
