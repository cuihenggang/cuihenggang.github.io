## Overview

A winter trip to China visiting my parents in 上海. On our way to 上海, we had an overnight connection in Vancouver. We stayed in China for about 4 weeks to wait for our F1 visa renewals. During that time, we made a 5-day trip to 云南.

Even though I grew up in China, this was actually my first time birding in China, so I saw a lot of lifers.

[eBird trip report](https://ebird.org/tripreport/155025).
