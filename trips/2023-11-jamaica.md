## Overview

A Thanksgiving holiday trip to Jamaica. We drove around the island, with stops at Montego Bay, Negril, Kingston, Ocho Rios and Falmouth. An important focus of this trip is to find the 28 endemic birds on the island, and I found 21 of them.

## Daily Itinerary

### Sat, November 18 — Arrival in Montego Bay

- Flights: WN 621 and WN 292, arriving at 2:50pm
- Hertz car rental

Stay: Hotel Grand A View, Montego Bay

### Sun, November 19 — Montego Bay to Negril

- Rocklands Bird Sanctuary
    - Highlights include Red-billed Streamertail, Jamaican Mango, etc.
- Half Moon Beach
- Drive to Negril (2 hr)

Stay: The Cliff Hotel Jamaica, Negril

### Mon, November 20 — Horse Riding & Rick's Cafe

- 8:30am: Horse Riding Tour
- Dinner at Rick's Cafe

Stay: The Cliff Hotel Jamaica, Negril

### Tue, November 21 — Negril to Kingston

- Floyd's Pelican Bar
    - A very cool floating restaurant on the sea
    - Drive to Parottee and take a boat
    - Call 876-809-4239 for a boat upon arrival
    - No restroom available
- Drive to Kingston (4 hr)

Stay: ROK Hotel Kingston, Kingston

### Wed, November 22 — Birding Tour in Kingston

- Birding Tour with [Arrowhead Birding Tours](https://arrowheadbirding.com/)
    - Led by Ricardo Miller, the #1 birder in Jamaica on eBird
    - Started at 4:30am before sunrise to search for Jamaican Owl
    - Morning at Hardwar Gap in Blue Mountain NP
    - Afternoon at Hope Botanical Gardens
    - Found 18 of the 28 Jamaican endemic birds during the tour. Highly recommended!

Stay: ROK Hotel Kingston, Kingston

### Thu, November 23 — Bob Marley Tour

- 10:00am–11:30am: Bob Marley Home Tour
    - 56 Hope Road, Kingston, Jamaica
- Drive to Ocho Rios (1.5 hr)

Stay: Sago Palm Hotel, Ocho Rios

### Fri, November 24 — White River Rafting & Iuminous Lagoon

- White River Rafting
- Green Grotto Cave
    - Saw a lot of Jamaican Fruit-eating Bats in the cave
- Birding at Stewart Town
    - For Jamaican Crow and Black-billed Parrot
- Drive to Falmouth (1.5 hr)
- 6:30pm: Glistening Waters at Glistening Waters Luminous Lagoon Hotel & Attraction

Stay: The Vista Inn, Falmouth

### Sat, November 25 — Departure

- 2:15pm: Departure on WN 273, connecting to WN 2697
