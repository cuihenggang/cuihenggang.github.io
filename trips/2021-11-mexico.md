## Overview

A Thanksgiving holiday trip to the Yucatán Peninsula of Mexico. We did birdwatching and visited Chichén-Itzá, one of the New Seven Wonders of the World.

[eBird trip report](https://ebird.org/tripreport/155008).

## Daily Itinerary

### Sat, November 20 — Arrival in Cancún

- Fly to Cancún
- Buy supplies

Stay: Renaissance Cancún Resort & Marina

### Sun, November 21 — Río Lagartos

- Drive to Río Lagartos
- 1pm-5pm: Río Lagartos Boat Birding Tour with [Guia Naturalista William Canto](https://www.facebook.com/williamcantoguide/services/?ref=page_internal)
- Lagunas Rosadas

Stay: Hotel Rio Lagartos

### Mon, November 22 — Chichén Itzá

- Drive to Valladolid
- Chichén Itzá
- Cenote Ik Kil

Stay: The Lodge at Chichen Itza

### Tue, November 23 — Zona Arqueológica Cobá

- Zona Arqueológica Cobá
    - A good place for birding as well
- Gran Cenote
    - A good place to find Mesoamerican Slider (Trachemys venusta)
- Drive to Tulum

Stay: Kimpton Aluna Tulum, an IHG Hotel

### Wed, November 24 — Zona Arqueológica Muyil

- Zona Arqueológica Muyil
    - A good place for birding as well
- Cenote Azul
- Drive to Cancún

Stay: Hilton Cancún, an All-Inclusive Resort

### Thu, November 25 — Isla Mujeres

- Isla Mujeres

Stay: Hilton Cancún, an All-Inclusive Resort

### Fri, November 26 — Hilton Cancún

- Relax at the resort

Stay: Hilton Cancún, an All-Inclusive Resort

### Sat, November 27 — Return to Pittsburgh

- Fly back to Pittsburgh
