## Overview

A summer trip to Canada's Rocky Mountains area. Starting and ending in Edmonton, we drove along the Icefields Parkway and visited Jasper, Banff, Yoho, Glacier, Mount Revelstoke and Elk Island national parks.

[Google Map Itinerary](https://goo.gl/maps/m9B3qBpB3xxcyHHQA).

[eBird trip report](https://ebird.org/tripreport/155089).
