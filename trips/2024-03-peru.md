## Overview

A one-week trip to Peru, visiting Machu Picch, one of the New Seven Wonders of the World.

[eBird trip report](https://ebird.org/tripreport/214458).

I did two birding tours, one in Lima with Gustavo Bautista and one in Cusco with [Wild Watch Peru](https://wildwatchperu.com/tours/birding-in-cusco-best-locations-full-day/). I also did birding on my own at Machu Picchu and Aguas Calientes.
